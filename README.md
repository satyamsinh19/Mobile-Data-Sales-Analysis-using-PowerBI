📱 Mobile Sales Data Analysis using Power BI
🎯 Project Title: Power BI Project – Mobile Sales Dashboard
🗂️ Dataset Used: Mobile Sales.xlsx
🎓 Focus: End-to-end business intelligence using Power BI – data modeling, DAX, and interactive dashboards

🧾 Project Overview
This project simulates a real-world business scenario of a Mobile Sales company operating across multiple cities in India. It includes a deep analysis of sales performance, customer behavior, brand comparison, and transaction trends using a single structured dataset.

The dashboard was created as part of the Power BI course by Satish Dhawale, with a focus on data transformation, visual analytics, and business insights.

🗃️ Key Metrics Tracked
Total Sales: ₹769 Million

Total Quantity Sold: 19,000+ units

Total Transactions: 4,000+

Average Price per Unit: ₹40,000

📈 Key Insights from Analysis
📍 Top Cities by Sales: Delhi, Mumbai, and Ranchi emerged as the highest revenue-generating cities, indicating urban sales dominance.

📆 Monthly Sales Trends: March and June showed sales peaks, hinting at possible promotional periods or seasonal demand.

📱 Brand Performance: Apple, Samsung, and OnePlus led in total sales and customer engagement, showing strong brand preference.

💳 Popular Payment Methods: UPI and card payments dominated, highlighting digital transaction trends.

⭐ Customer Ratings: A majority of 5-star ratings suggest high customer satisfaction with both product and service quality.
